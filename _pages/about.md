I am a Machine Learning PhD student at Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science, advised by Professor [Bryan Wilder](https://bryanwilder.github.io). My research focuses on leveraging machine learning and optimization to develop AI for equitable, data-driven decision-making under uncertainty. My ultimate goal is to apply my work to real-world problems affecting the less fortunate; I am deeply passionate about maximizing the societal impact of my research. In a past life I was a Machine learning engineer at [Factored.ai](https://www.factored.ai). I hold a B.Sc. and a M.Sc., both in Mathematics from Universidad de Los Andes (2017, 2018).
